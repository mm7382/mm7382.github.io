01

The five steps

One: find the Day Master, note phase and polarity. Two: the Day branch’s main stem — one sentence on intimacy. Three: the month pillar — one sentence on work.

Four: count the five groups — one sentence on default and homework. Five: note combinations and clashes.

02

A worked example

Say the Day Master is Xin Metal (yin), the Day branch’s main stem Yi Wood (Indirect Wealth), the month stem Ren Water (Hurting Officer), with Output largest and Officer smallest.

Reading: fine-grained and decisive. In intimacy you work through fluid resources and chances — warm, easily distracted. At work you push through expression and output and dislike being managed. Homework: set your own boundaries or burn out.

03

Five sentences are enough

A complete reading is accurate, not long. Five sentences cover the main outline.

If you cannot say one, go back to that lesson rather than covering it with vague adjectives.

Five steps, five sentences. If one will not come, go back to that lesson.
