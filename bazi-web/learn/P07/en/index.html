LESSON P07 / 10 OF 20

Generation and control

Generation and control are push and pull, not good and bad.

Original app lesson · freely available here

01

Generation: one feeds the next

Wood feeds Fire, Fire feeds Earth, Earth feeds Metal, Metal feeds Water, Water feeds Wood. The receiver gains; the giver spends.

So generating is not purely good. Someone always generating gets drained.

02

Control: one checks the next

Wood checks Earth, Earth checks Water, Water checks Fire, Fire checks Metal, Metal checks Wood. The checked is limited; the checker spends effort.

Control is not bad. Without it a person scatters. Deadlines and duty are control made concrete.

03

Applying it

Find your Day Master’s phase and ask: who feeds me, whom do I feed, who checks me.

Answer those and you have your daily energy flow. A missing one is usually where life feels stuck.

Generating drains, control shapes. Ask who feeds me, whom I feed, who checks me.
