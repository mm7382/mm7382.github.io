LESSON P04 / 07 OF 20

Reading with an unknown birth time

An unknown hour is not a broken chart. Here is what you can and cannot read.

Original app lesson · freely available here

01

What still reads

The Day Master stands, so who you are and how you push still reads. The month pillar stands, so upbringing and home ground read too.

With the year pillar as well, three of four give you the main outline.

02

What to set aside

Later life, children and the private self lean on the hour pillar. Without it, hold those conclusions.

Do not fill the gap with a guess. Wrong data produces a person who does not exist.

03

How to recover it

Birth certificates and hospital records often record the time. Asking an elder “was it light out?” works better than asking for a clock time.

Add it later and nothing you already read is overturned.

Read one block less rather than inventing data. Three pillars already give the outline.
