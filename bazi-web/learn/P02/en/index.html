LESSON P02 / 05 OF 20

Solar terms and month boundaries

The Bazi month follows solar terms, not the calendar.

Original app lesson · freely available here

01

Months turn on solar terms

The month pillar turns on a major solar term — Start of Spring, Waking of Insects, Clear and Bright — not on the first of a month.

Births on 3 and 5 February can sit in different month pillars because Start of Spring falls between them.

02

The year too

The Bazi year turns at Start of Spring, not at Lunar New Year and not on 1 January. Births just before the new year often carry the previous year pillar.

This is a calendar definition, and the app already applies it.

03

Why it matters for reading

The month pillar is often read as a person’s home ground: upbringing, and where they work hardest and best. One pillar off changes that.

For births near a term boundary, confirm before drawing conclusions.

Years turn at Start of Spring and months at major terms. Confirm boundary births before reading.
