The yearly palace: where this year starts

Know that the year's Life Palace is the palace whose branch matches this year's.

Original app lesson · free on the web

This lesson has been self-reviewed and is awaiting final sign-off. The classical passages have been checked word by word; the plain-language text and the exercises are written by this project.

Which cell do I start from for this year?
01 · plain

In plain words

The year layer is about this year. Whatever this year's branch is, the palace with the same branch is the year's Life Palace. 2026 is a Bing Wu year, so the year's Life Palace is the palace at Wu. It is the same rule for everyone, whatever their birth data.

02 · classic

What the text says

The text calls the year Tai Sui. Volume 3 says that for Tai Sui one looks at its trines and opposite: the year layer is also read with its four positions.
