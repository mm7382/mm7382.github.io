Dou Jun and the monthly palace: where this month starts

Know how Dou Jun is found, and how this month's Life Palace is counted from it.

Original app lesson · free on the web

Which cell do I start from for this month?
01 · plain

In plain words

Dou Jun is where the months start, the first lunar month. Count the year's Life Palace as month one and go backward to your birth month; treat that palace as the Zi hour and count forward to your birth hour; where you stop is Dou Jun. The second lunar month is the next palace, and so on; a leap month uses the same number.

02 · classic

Classical sources
  1. 於流年太歲宮起正月逆至本生月，又從本生月起子順數至本生時安斗君。

    From the palace of the year's Tai Sui, count the first month and go backward to the birth month; from there count the Zi hour forward to the birth hour, and place the month marker.

    Zi Wei Dou Shu Quan Shu, Volume 2, "Placing Dou Jun"; traditionally attributed in received editions
