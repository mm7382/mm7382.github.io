Workshop: reading an unfamiliar chart

Use three steps to pick out the frame of an unfamiliar chart in a minute.

Original app lesson · free on the web

This lesson has been self-reviewed and is awaiting final sign-off. The classical passages have been checked word by word; the plain-language text and the exercises are written by this project.

Handed an unfamiliar chart, how do you see its frame in one minute?
01 · plain

Three steps

Step one, find the Life Palace and its branch. Step two, find which palace holds the Body Palace. Step three, see which major stars the Life Palace lists. All three are reading only, no explaining. After them, the frame of the chart is clear.

02 · classic

What the text says

The Gu Sui Fu says that once the body marker is placed, the foundation is known. That is why the three steps start with the Life and Body palaces.

03 · inHouse

Why the frame comes first

Every later lesson builds on the frame: palace topics, star descriptions, the opposite palace and the four positions. If the frame is misread, everything after it is wrong, so practise the three steps first.

04 · inHouse

Compare with your own chart

Do the three steps on your own chart, read them out loud, and time yourself for a minute.

05 · inHouse

An everyday example

For a chart screenshot, start with whose chart it is, its birth data and the displayed time layer, not the most striking star. Missing settings should remain questions, not guesses.
