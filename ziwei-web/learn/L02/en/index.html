The Life Palace and the Body Palace

Find the Life Palace and the Body Palace on the chart, and know that both are starting markers for reading.

Original app lesson · free on the web

This lesson has been self-reviewed and is awaiting final sign-off. The classical passages have been checked word by word; the plain-language text and the exercises are written by this project.

If both are about me, why does the chart have a Life Palace and a Body Palace too?
01 · plain

In plain words

The Life Palace is the first stop in reading, and is about the person themself. The Body Palace is another marker that falls in one of the twelve palaces. It only ever falls in one of six: the Life, Partner, Wealth, Travel, Career or Inner Life palace, and sometimes in the same cell as the Life Palace. Find these two markers first, then read on.

02 · classic

What the text says

The Tai Wei Fu treats the body and life markers together, calling them the root of the reading. The classical texts name them as one pair, so they are usually read together.

03 · inHouse

Compare with your own chart

Open the Chart tab. The Life Palace cell is highlighted, and the cell holding the Body Palace is marked Body. Say one sentence: my Body Palace falls in such-and-such palace.

04 · inHouse

An everyday example

You can care about your way of approaching life while devoting much energy to work, family or relationships. Life and Body are two starting points, not competing personalities. Note the Body palace's life topic before comparing it with experience.

Record the Life and Body positions separately rather than treating them as two personalities.

TEACHING EXAMPLE

On the teaching example the Life Palace is at Xu with Po Jun inside, and the Body Palace is at Shen, which is the Partner Palace. So this chart's Body Palace falls in the Partner Palace, not in the Life Palace cell.
