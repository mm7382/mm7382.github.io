The minor period: one palace a year

Know that the yearly age palace moves one palace a year, starting from the birth year's branch, forward for men and backward for women.

Original app lesson · free on the web

This lesson has been self-reviewed and is awaiting final sign-off. The classical passages have been checked word by word; the plain-language text and the exercises are written by this project.

If a decade only changes every ten years, what do I look at each year?
01 · plain

In plain words

The age palace moves one palace a year by nominal age. Its start depends on the birth year's branch: Yin, Wu or Xu years start at Chen; Shen, Zi or Chen at Xu; Si, You or Chou at Wei; Hai, Mao or Wei at Chou. Then men count forward and women backward, whatever the year's yin or yang.

02 · classic

What the text says

The four starting points and the rule that men go forward and women backward both come from Volume 2's rule on the age palace.

04 · inHouse

An everyday example

Minor-period and yearly layers may point to different cells in the same year. They use different starting rules; do not silently choose the answer you prefer.

Name the minor-period or yearly layer explicitly in your notes.

TEACHING EXAMPLE

The example is a woman born in a Wu year: age 1 starts at Chen and counts backward; at 37 it is back at Chen, the Travel Palace.
