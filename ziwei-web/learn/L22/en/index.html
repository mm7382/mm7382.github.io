01 · plain

In plain words

A good note shows where it comes from. Chart facts, such as Zi Wei being in the Travel Palace, come from the calculation; classical text needs its volume and section; this app's rules, such as the modern decade method, should be marked as the app's choice. Keep the three apart so you can tell them apart later.
