Workshop: finding where no conclusion can be drawn

Point out where this app cannot yet draw a conclusion on a chart, and say why.

Original app lesson · free on the web

This lesson has been self-reviewed and is awaiting final sign-off. The classical passages have been checked word by word; the plain-language text and the exercises are written by this project.

Websites say my star is in a strong position. Why doesn't the app say so?
01 · plain

In plain words

You can now say which palace, which stars, and which palaces are opposite and in the four positions. Some things this app cannot conclude: star brightness, the meaning of the transformations, and minor or harsh stars are not computed or reviewed in this version. Stop at observation there, and say the app draws no conclusion on that point.

02 · classic

What the text says

The Tai Wei Fu asks the reader to understand how stars restrain each other and whether they sit in their own place before judging; this app does not compute those yet.

03 · inHouse

Compare with your own chart

Look at your Life Palace: name its stars, then name one thing the app does not conclude, such as the star's brightness.

04 · inHouse

An everyday example

A chart alone cannot answer whether a relationship will end. Reframe the question around communication habits worth observing, leaving real decisions with the people involved.

  1. 星有同躔，數有分定，須明其生剋之要，必詳乎得垣失度之分。

    Stars share the same path, and the method has its own divisions; one must understand how they generate and restrain one another, and look closely at whether a star sits in its own place or out of it.

    Zi Wei Dou Shu Quan Shu, Volume 1, "Tai Wei Fu"; traditionally attributed in received editions
