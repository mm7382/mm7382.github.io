Workshop: comparing two charts' Life, Body and empty palaces

Put your chart beside the teaching example and compare only visible facts: the Life Palace, the Body Palace and the empty palaces.

Original app lesson · free on the web

This lesson has been self-reviewed and is awaiting final sign-off. The classical passages have been checked word by word; the plain-language text and the exercises are written by this project.

With two charts side by side, how do you compare without ranking them?
01 · plain

In plain words

When comparing two charts, compare only what you can see: the branch of the Life Palace, the palace holding the Body Palace, and which palaces have no major star. No better or worse, no ranking, no saying one chart beats the other. Two charts differ only because the birth details differ.

02 · classic

What the text says

The Tai Wei Fu says the stars are spread over twelve enclosures: every chart has the same twelve cells. Because the cells are the same, two charts have common positions to compare.

03 · inHouse

How to practise

First write three lines of facts about the example, then three about your own chart, then set them side by side and see what matches and what differs. One fact per line, such as: the Life Palace is at Xu.

04 · inHouse

05 · inHouse

An everyday example

Two friends having different Life palaces does not rank their charts. Fix the comparison first: positions and major-star counts, not success or desirability.

Compare matching fields in both charts without ranking the people.

TEACHING EXAMPLE

Three facts about the example: the Life Palace is at Xu; the Body Palace falls in the Partner Palace; the Children and Siblings palaces have no major star. Put your three lines beside these to see what matches.
