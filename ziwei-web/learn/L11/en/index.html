Career, Property, Inner Life and Parents

Know which part of life the Career, Property, Inner Life and Parents palaces each cover.

Original app lesson · free on the web

This lesson has been self-reviewed and is awaiting final sign-off. The classical passages have been checked word by word; the plain-language text and the exercises are written by this project.

Which cells do I look at for work, and for home?
01 · plain

In plain words

Career covers work and the way you get things done; Property covers home and the feel of the household; Inner Life covers enjoyment and peace of mind; Parents covers parents, elders and people above you. Fu De is rendered Inner Life because the usual English words carry a sense of good or bad outcome.

02 · classic

What the text says

Volume 2 lists the twelve palaces in order from the Life Palace. The text says wife and concubine, and servants; the app shows the Partner Palace and the Friends Palace. Those are modern wordings, not the original terms.

03 · inHouse

Compare with your own chart

Find these four on your chart and read out their major stars. With the eight from the last two lessons, that is all twelve.

04 · inHouse

An everyday example

Thinking about work after hours may raise questions about responsibility, home routines and rest. Read Career, Property, Inner Life and Parents as distinct topics rather than letting Career explain everything.

Record work arrangements, living space and rest needs separately.
