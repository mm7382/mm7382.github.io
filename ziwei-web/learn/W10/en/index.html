Do this month and this year use the same cell?
01 · plain

In plain words

The month and the year are separate layers: the year changes yearly, the month monthly. Always say which layer you mean, for example: this month's Life Palace is my Wealth Palace; do not mix it up with the year.

02 · classic

What the text says

The months are counted from Dou Jun, placed by Volume 2's rule on Dou Jun.

03 · inHouse

Compare with your own chart

Say which palace is this month's Life Palace, then which is this year's, as two separate sentences.

TEACHING EXAMPLE

On 12 October (the ninth lunar month) the example's month Life Palace is at Wu, the same as the year's. That is a coincidence; another month differs, so keep the two sentences apart.

Classical sources
  1. 於流年太歲宮起正月逆至本生月，又從本生月起子順數至本生時安斗君。

    From the palace of the year's Tai Sui, count the first month and go backward to the birth month; from there count the Zi hour forward to the birth hour, and place the month marker.

    Zi Wei Dou Shu Quan Shu, Volume 2, "Placing Dou Jun"; traditionally attributed in received editions
