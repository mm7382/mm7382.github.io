Is there an example of reading a whole chart?
01 · plain

In plain words

This workshop uses the teaching example as the case. It is fictional birth data run through the real engine; it is not a real person or a celebrity, since reliable, authorised birth hours for public figures are almost impossible to get. Read it once in the order from lesson 31, stating chart facts at each step.

02 · classic

What the text says

The order follows Volume 3: body and life first, then wealth, career and travel.

03 · inHouse

Compare with your own chart

After the example, say which palaces on your chart hold two major stars, to prepare for reading your own chart next.

04 · inHouse

An everyday example

A fictional teaching character is not a real person to guess. Use the supplied information without inventing a job, relationship or history, and keep examples separate from user data.

Use only the example's stated information; do not invent missing history.

TEACHING EXAMPLE

A whole reading of the example: Life at Xu (Po Jun), Body in Partner at Shen (Lian Zhen), Earth Five; the four positions add Travel at Chen (Zi Wei, Tian Xiang), Wealth at Wu (Qi Sha) and Career at Yin (Tan Lang); Siblings and Children are empty; on brightness and transformations the app draws no conclusion.
