One chart, different points of focus

Know that different classical sections start reading from different places, so one chart can be approached from different angles.

Original app lesson · free on the web

This lesson has been self-reviewed and is awaiting final sign-off. The classical passages have been checked word by word; the plain-language text and the exercises are written by this project.

Two books read the chart differently. Which do I follow?
01 · plain

In plain words

The text does not have only one starting point: Volume 3 starts from the body and life, while the Gu Sui Fu says to look at the Inner Life Palace first. Neither is simply right; the angle differs. When reading a chart, say which palace you are starting from, so others know your angle.

02 · classic

What the text says

The Gu Sui Fu puts the Inner Life Palace first; Volume 3 puts body and life first. Both lines are below.

03 · inHouse

Compare with your own chart

Read your chart once from the Inner Life Palace: name its stars. Then once from the Life Palace, and compare.

Classical sources
  1. 第一先看福德，再三細考遷移

    First look at the Inner Life Palace, and examine the Travel Palace again and again.

    Zi Wei Dou Shu Quan Shu, Volume 1, "Gu Sui Fu"; traditionally attributed in received editions
  2. 男命先看身命，次看財帛宮祿遷移

    For a man's chart, look first at the body and life, then at wealth, career and travel.

    Zi Wei Dou Shu Quan Shu, Volume 3; traditionally attributed in received editions
