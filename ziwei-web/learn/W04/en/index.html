Workshop: the five elements phase and where it is shown

Find the five elements phase on the chart, and know that its number is the age at which the first decade begins.

What is the phase named at the top of the chart, and what has it to do with me?
01 · plain

In plain words

Every chart has one five elements phase, out of five: Water Two, Wood Three, Metal Four, Earth Five and Fire Six. The number in the name is the age at which the first decade begins; for Earth Five, the first decade starts at five. The phase is a classification from working out the chart, not good or bad.

02 · classic

What the text says

The chart diagrams in Volume 2 are laid out by these five phases. What is quoted here is a diagram heading, not a passage of argument.

03 · inHouse

Compare with your own chart

The Chart tab shows your five elements phase near the top. Note its number: it is the age your first decade begins, which a later lesson on decades will use.

04 · inHouse

An everyday example

Earth phase 5 is a chart classification, not an abundance of earth or a fifth-place ranking. Recognising the field is enough here; understanding your chart does not require learning manual placement.

Record the phase as a classification, not a score.

TEACHING EXAMPLE

The example is Earth Five, so its first decade begins at age five; each decade lasts ten years.
