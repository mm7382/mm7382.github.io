Workshop: finding this year's life palace on your chart

Find this year's Life Palace on your chart, and map love, work and money onto natal palaces.

Original app lesson · free on the web

This lesson has been self-reviewed and is awaiting final sign-off. The classical passages have been checked word by word; the plain-language text and the exercises are written by this project.

This year, which cells do I look at for love, work and money?
01 · plain

In plain words

Find the year's Life Palace, then from it count the year's Partner, Career and Wealth palaces in the usual order. Each falls on one of your natal palaces, and you read that palace's stars. This is a structural reading, not good or bad.

02 · classic

What the text says

Volume 3: for Tai Sui, look at its trines and opposite; the year is read with the same relations between palaces as the natal chart.

03 · inHouse

Compare with your own chart

Say which palace is this year's Life Palace, then open the This Year analysis and compare the three topic palaces it lists.

04 · inHouse

An everyday example

Compare this year's and last year's starting positions. If birth data or natal stars also differ, check whether another person's chart is selected before treating the difference as a yearly change.

Confirm identical birth data before comparing years.

TEACHING EXAMPLE

This year the example's Life Palace is at Wu (natal Wealth). Counting from Wu, the year's Partner is at Chen (natal Travel: Zi Wei, Tian Xiang), Wealth at Yin (natal Career: Tan Lang), and Career at Xu (natal Life: Po Jun).
