Mercury: Thinking and Speaking

By the end you will know which side of you Mercury describes, and why it always stays near the Sun.

Approved original lessons, free on the web · 2026-10-12

THE IDEA

Mercury is about how you think and talk, and it never strays far from the Sun.

01

Mercury is about how you think and how you say it.

What Mercury is about

Mercury works like “head plus mouth”: how you take news in, think it over and say it out loud. Do you talk first and think later, or think it through before you speak? Do you go straight to the point, or tell the whole story?

Later interpretation

It describes a habit. Wherever Mercury falls, each way of thinking and talking has what it does easily, and you can use that to know yourself.

Later interpretation

What the old texts say: in Book I ch. 4 of the Tetrabiblos, Ptolemy says only two things about Mercury — it stays near the Sun, and it changes quickly. Book III ch. 13 gives it the rational, thinking part of the soul.

Cited source
02

Your Mercury sign is your Sun sign or the one next door.

Why it stays by the Sun

Mercury's orbit lies closer to the Sun than ours, so from Earth it always circles near the Sun. In signs, that means your Mercury is in your Sun sign or in the sign just before or after it.

Derived calculation

That helps explain something familiar: two Libras can talk very differently, and the difference often lies in which side Mercury fell on. With Mercury also in Libra, speech minds the mood first; with Mercury in Scorpio, speech is more guarded, watching before speaking.

Later interpretation

What the old texts say: Book I ch. 17 of the Tetrabiblos says Mercury “never moves more than one sign from the Sun in either direction.”

Cited source

Astronomy: Mercury goes round the Sun in about 88 days, the fastest planet in the solar system (NASA figure).

Cited source
03

Look at your habits of handling things.

Reading your own Mercury

Find which sign your Mercury is in, then compare it with your Sun: the same sign, or next door?

Reflection prompt

With Mercury in the Sun's sign, what you think and what you show tend to match; with Mercury next door, people often feel “what I think and what comes out are not quite the same.”

Later interpretation

Checking is simple. Hold it up against the last real conversation you had. Does it fit? Note where it fits and where it does not; both are clues to who you are.

Reflection prompt

What the old texts say: Book III ch. 13 of the Tetrabiblos warns that judgements like this need many conditions weighed together before any conclusion.

Cited source

LOOK CLOSER

A common mix-up

“Mercury shows how smart you are.”

Mercury is about your habits of thinking and talking, and every one of those habits has what it does well.

“Mercury can be in any sign.”

It always stays near the Sun, so it is your Sun sign or the sign just before or after.

“Mercury is retrograde, so don't discuss anything today.”

This app marks retrograde so you know that Mercury appears to move backwards for a while. As NASA explains, that is an effect of Earth's own motion; the planet itself keeps moving forward along its orbit. Lesson 19 covers it.

Limits of this lesson

The retrograde mark tells you Mercury appears to move backwards while the planet itself keeps moving forward; astrology is a language for describing yourself, not a guide to what to do on which day.

The example uses fixed demonstration placements — not a real person and not computed from your data.
