Organize Your Notes

By the end your notes will be in a shape you can still understand when you come back, and you will know where they are kept and what would delete them.

Approved original lessons, free on the web · 2026-10-12

THE IDEA

Your task: tidy your notes and check where they are kept.

01

When you tidy your notes, keep the dates, the bases and the doubts.

Tidying so you can come back

Records are only worth comparing if you can tell them apart. So keep three things when you tidy. First, dates: six months on, that is how you tell what you wrote then from what you changed later. Second, the basis marks: computed, traditional, or my own observation. Third, the doubts: candidates, descriptions that missed, things you have not worked out yet.

Reflection prompt

Keep those three and every line can be traced to its source and compared six months on — that is exactly what makes notes useful.

Reflection prompt

What the old texts say: Book I ch. 2 of Ptolemy's Tetrabiblos says one reason predictions fail is that the examples they rest on are not alike.

LOOK CLOSER

A common mix-up

“The shorter the notes, the better.”

Keep the dates, bases and doubts, and a note can be traced to its source and compared later.

“My notes disappear when my membership lapses.”

A lapsed membership only locks the member lessons again; it deletes no notes, and your progress stays too. What really clears them is delete all data in settings.
