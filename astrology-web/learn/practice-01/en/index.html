Build Your First Chart

By the end your own chart is set up in this app, and you know what each field you filled in actually changes.

Approved original lessons, free on the web · 2026-10-12

THE IDEA

Task: set up your own chart and find out what each field changes.

01

Date, time, place and time zone each change your chart.

What the four fields do

The date fixes which sign the Sun and most planets are in. The time fixes the Ascendant, the Midheaven and the twelve houses; fill it in and that layer appears. The place plus the time zone fix what the clock said where you were at that moment.

How this tool works

One time zone out moves the whole chart an hour earlier or later. So a wrong time zone matters as much as a wrong birth time.

Derived calculation

This app’s city search brings the time zone with it, which is safer than typing one in yourself.

How this tool works

What the old texts say: in Book III ch. 2 of the Tetrabiblos, Ptolemy takes the degree rising on the eastern horizon at the moment of birth as the chart’s starting point, so both the moment and the place are needed.

Cited source
02

Once the chart is set up, check your typing, the blanks and the candidates, then start reading.

Three checks once it is set up

Once the chart exists, check three things. First, go back over the date, time and place you typed. Second, look at the Ascendant row. Third, see whether any body is marked “unconfirmed” with two candidates.

Reflection prompt

If you entered no time, the Ascendant row stays empty, which is exactly the correct result. A body marked “unconfirmed” changed sign on the day you were born. You can edit your data in settings at any time, and the whole chart is worked out again.

How this tool works

The two most common typing slips are mixing up morning and afternoon (2:30 and 14:30 are twelve hours apart) and swapping month and day (4 March entered as 3 April). With those three checked, your chart is solid material to work with.

Teaching example

Demonstration data

Enter 1990-03-21, Taipei, time unknown: the Sun is Pisces or Aries, two candidates.

Add 14:30 to the same day: the Sun settles on one sign, and the Ascendant and houses appear.

Set the time zone to Tokyo by mistake and the chart moves an hour; the Ascendant may change sign.

Fixed demonstration data: same day, same place, changing only the time or the time zone shows what each field does. · This is a teaching example, not your chart.

LOOK CLOSER

A common mix-up

“The time zone hardly matters — it is the same day either way.”

One zone out is an hour out. The Ascendant changes sign roughly every two hours, and the whole set of houses moves with it.

“An empty Ascendant row means the app is broken.”

With no birth time, empty is the correct result; once you add a time, the Ascendant and houses appear.

Limits of this lesson

Without a birth time the Ascendant, Midheaven and houses stay empty until you add a time.

The name is not used in any calculation, and you do not type the time zone yourself.

The demonstration data is not a real person and not a model answer.
