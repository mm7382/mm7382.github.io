Lunar Phase and the Sun–Moon Pair

By the end you will know that a moon phase is simply the angle between the Sun and Moon, be able to see from your own chart which stretch of the phases you were born in, and recognise a new and a full moon.

Approved original lessons, free on the web · 2026-10-12

THE IDEA

A moon phase is how far apart the Sun and Moon are: full moon is an opposition, new moon a conjunction.

01

A phase is how many degrees the Moon is from the Sun.

A phase is an angle

A phase is the aspect idea from earlier lessons, applied to the Sun and Moon: new moon is a conjunction (around 0 degrees), full moon an opposition (around 180), and the quarters are close to a square (90). The further the Moon moves on from the Sun, the more of it is lit, until it is fully lit when opposite.

Derived calculation

By NASA's figures, one full round of phases (the synodic month) takes about 29.53 days, and one orbit of the Moon about 27.3217 days.

Cited source

The round of phases is a little longer than one orbit because the Sun has moved on as well: when the Moon finishes its orbit the Sun has moved ahead, and the Moon needs a little over two more days to catch up.

Derived calculation

What the old texts say: Ptolemy's Tetrabiblos, Book I ch. 8, splits the month into four by phase — moist from new moon to first quarter, hot from first quarter to full, dry from full to last quarter, cold from last quarter until the Moon disappears. The text uses phase as a relation between where the Sun and Moon are. Book III ch. 2 also uses the word syzygy for a new or full moon.

Cited source
02

A new moon is the Sun and Moon meeting, a full moon them facing each other; the old text used them as reference points for correcting a birth time.

New moon and full moon

A new moon is often read as a time for beginnings, and a full moon as a time for seeing results and letting go. Many people write down what they want to start at a new moon, then look back at the full moon to see how far it has come.

Later interpretation

Spotting them is easy: Sun and Moon in or near the same sign is the new-moon stretch; the Moon in the sign opposite the Sun is the full-moon stretch. With the Sun in Libra, for example, the Moon reaching Aries is close to full.

Derived calculation

The chart page shows the Sun and Moon at your moment of birth and the aspect between them; from that aspect you can tell whether you were born at a new moon, a full moon, or somewhere in between.

How this tool works

What the old texts say: Book III ch. 2 first notes that birth times taken from water clocks and sundials often err. To fix the degree of the birth moment, take the syzygy — new or full moon — just before the birth, work out its degree exactly (both lights at a new moon, the one above the horizon at a full moon), then see which stars rule that place at the birth. So in the old texts new and full moons are reference points for a calculation.

Cited source
03

What you look at is how far apart your Sun and Moon are.

Reading your own Sun and Moon

The birth phase is often read as “how far your inner needs sit from the way you come across”: near a new moon the two point the same way, so what you want and what you show are close; near a full moon they face each other, and you often look for a balance between the two.

Later interpretation

You can check it yourself: how many signs apart are your Sun and Moon? Zero or one is roughly the new-moon stretch; six is the full-moon stretch; about three is near a quarter. Sun in Leo and Moon in Aquarius, for example, are six apart: the full-moon stretch.

Reflection prompt

The Moon moves about 12° a day. So without a birth time the number of signs apart is usually still clear, but the exact angle needs the time.

Derived calculation

Without a birth time, this app shows the range of the angle across your whole birth day instead: the two ends of the range are the earliest and latest angles possible that day.

How this tool works

What the old texts say: Book I ch. 8 uses phase to split up qualities, in order moist, hot, dry and cold.

Cited source

LOOK CLOSER

A common mix-up

“Wish on a new moon and it will come true.”

A new moon is an angle between the Sun and Moon. Writing down what you want to start at a new moon and looking back at the full moon is a good habit for sorting your thoughts; whether it happens depends on what you do.

“Phases are a separate system.”

A phase is just the angle between Sun and Moon: new moon a conjunction, full moon an opposition.

“One orbit of the Moon is one round of phases.”

One orbit is about 27.3217 days and one round of phases about 29.53, because the Sun has moved on too.

Limits of this lesson

Without a birth time, the angle between the Sun and Moon is shown as a range across your whole birth day, not a single number.

Reading the phases is a language for describing yourself, not a prediction, and promises no wish will come true.
