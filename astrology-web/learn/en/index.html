Notes & progress ↗
  1. 01 / COREWhat a Birth Chart IsA birth chart is a table of where things were in the sky at one moment, seen from one place.✓
  2. 02 / CORESun, Moon, AscendantThree placements, three different sides — read them together.✓
  3. 03 / COREThe Language of the Twelve SignsA sign is an adjective: it tells you how.✓
  4. 04 / COREMercury: Thinking and SpeakingMercury is about how you think and talk, and it never strays far from the Sun.✓
  5. 05 / COREVenus: Liking and ValuingVenus is about what you like and what feels worth having.✓
  6. 06 / COREMars: Action and DriveMars is about how you push and how you get angry.✓
  7. 07 / COREJupiter and Saturn: Expansion and LimitJupiter and Saturn move slowly; Jupiter shows where you reach out, Saturn where you set limits.✓
  8. 08 / COREOuter Planets: The Generational BackdropThe outer planets move very slowly and describe the backdrop your generation shares.✓
  9. 09 / COREWhat Houses AreSigns divide the sky; houses divide what you see from where you were born.✓
  10. 10 / COREHouses 1–4: Self and RootsHouses one to four: yourself, what you own, the people around you, your roots.✓
  11. 11 / COREHouses 5–8: Relating and SharingHouses five to eight: what you put out, what you look after daily, the person opposite you, and what you share with others.✓
  12. 12 / COREHouses 9–12: World and InteriorHouses nine to twelve: what you reach for, how you are seen, the people with you, and where you trip yourself up.✓
  13. 13 / COREWhat Aspects AreAn aspect is how many degrees apart two bodies are; the four main ones are 180, 120, 90 and 60 degrees.✓
  14. 14 / COREConjunction and OppositionIn a conjunction two bodies sit together; in an opposition they face each other across the chart.✓
  15. 15 / CORETrine and SquareA trine joins one element group, like an easy gift; a square joins different kinds, like homework to practise.✓
  16. 16 / CORESextile and OrbA sextile is two planets 60 degrees apart; the orb decides how far off still counts, and this app uses 4 degrees for a birth-chart sextile.✓
  17. 17 / COREElements and ModalitiesTwelve signs are four elements times three ways of moving — one sign per cell.✓
  18. 18 / CORERulership and Dignity in TraditionDomicile is a planet's own sign; exaltation and depression are another pair of opposite places. One table shows them all.✓
  19. 19 / COREHow to Read RetrogradeRetrograde means “seen from Earth, its degrees are going backwards” — the way Earth's own motion makes it look.✓
  20. 20 / CORELunar Phase and the Sun–Moon PairA moon phase is how far apart the Sun and Moon are: full moon is an opposition, new moon a conjunction.✓
  21. 21 / COREWhen the Birth Time Is UncertainWithout a time, signs and aspects still hold; with a time, the Ascendant and house layer appear.✓
  22. 22 / COREWhat Transits AreA transit is today's planet positions held up against the positions at your birth.✓
  23. 23 / COREAstrology Is Not PredictionEven Ptolemy wrote that predictions sometimes fail, and that birthplace, upbringing and custom shape a person too. A chart is most useful for giving you words to describe yourself and questions to ask yourself.✓
  24. 24 / COREReading a Whole Chart as a PersonRead a chart in order: first what data you have, then three key lines, then where they disagree.✓
