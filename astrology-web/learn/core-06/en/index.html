Mars: Action and Drive

By the end you will know which side of you Mars describes, how long it stays in a sign, and how to check the force it stands for against your own life.

Approved original lessons, free on the web · 2026-10-12

THE IDEA

Mars is about how you push and how you get angry.

01

Mars is about how you push and how you get angry.

What Mars is about

Mars is about drive, push and the way you get angry. Faced with something unfair, do you speak up on the spot, or hold it in and think it over at home? With a hard job, do you charge through in one go, or push a little every day?

Later interpretation

When you look at your own Mars, ask two things: where does this force usually go? Can you put it down afterwards? You can answer both from your own recent experience.

Reflection prompt

What the old texts say: in Book I ch. 4 of the Tetrabiblos, Ptolemy says the nature of Mars is mainly to dry and to burn, in keeping with its fiery colour and its nearness to the Sun’s sphere. Ch. 5 counts Mars and Saturn as maleficent — Mars for too much dryness, Saturn for too much cold; the label rests on whether a nature is mild or extreme. Book IV ch. 4 makes Mars one of the three planets to look at for what kind of work a person does.

Cited source
02

Mars changes sign about every two months on average.

How fast Mars moves

Mars goes round the Sun in about 686.980 days (NASA figure). Shared across twelve signs, that is about two months a sign. It is an average — retrograde motion can take it back and forth inside one sign, so a single stay can be longer.

Derived calculation

So a Mars sign changes far more slowly than the Moon’s, and people born in the same month often share one. To tell people apart, add the house it fell in, and that needs a birth time.

Derived calculation / Later interpretation

What the old texts say: Ptolemy talks about the qualities of Mars and its positions relative to the other bodies.

Cited source
03

Mars is about how you push.

Reading your own Mars

Mars describes how you push. Hands on first, or terms first? More force when blocked, or a different route? When angry, does it come out or stay in? It describes the way you push.

Later interpretation

Mars and the Sun can point different ways: the side the Sun wants seen and the place you are willing to put in effort may be two separate things.

Later interpretation

When you check yourself, looking at the gap between the two is often more useful than the Mars sign alone. Ask yourself: is what you most want to put effort into the same as the self you want others to see?

Reflection prompt

What the old texts say: Book IV ch. 4 of the Tetrabiblos places Mars inside the question of what kind of work a person does, together with Venus and Mercury, and adds that the sign it is in matters.

Cited source

Teaching example

A demonstration chart

Sun in Libra, Mars in Capricorn: polite when talking, but pushes a job right to the end.

Move Mars to Cancer: the effort goes into protecting people first, the task second.

Same Sun, a completely different way of pushing — the difference is Mars.

LOOK CLOSER

A common mix-up

“Mars is malefic, so Mars things go badly.”

“Malefic” means an extreme nature — too much dryness. When you read your own Mars, look at where the force goes and whether you can put it down afterwards.

“A Mars sign shows how big someone's temper is.”

Mars describes the way you push. People born in the same month often share a Mars sign, so telling them apart also takes the house it fell in.

CHECK YOURSELF

Now, try it yourself.

1. Which side of you does Mars mainly describe?

2. On average, how long does Mars stay in one sign?
