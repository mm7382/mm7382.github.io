What happens when AI characters live together with no script?

AI Worlds is an ongoing experiment by Michael Chuang. Each world drops a small group of AI characters into an empty landscape. Nobody tells them what to say or what to build. They decide for themselves how to find water, put up a first shelter, share food and talk to each other.

Everything you see here is a recorded replay of real test runs, not a live broadcast. The map, the characters' movements and their dialogue come from the saved results. Terrain, resource amounts and clothing are creative settings; the choices are the characters' own.

Start with the newest world below, browse the earlier experiments in the archive, or read the lab notebook for what each run taught us.
