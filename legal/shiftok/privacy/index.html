我們蒐集什麼

開發者不蒐集你的 App 資料。你建立的店家、員工、班表、出勤、請假與薪資資料，由 App 保存在自己的 iPhone 上。這個 App 沒有開發者伺服器或帳號系統，不會自行把這些資料傳到開發者雲端。

若你啟用 iPhone 的系統備份，Apple 可能依裝置設定備份 App 資料；這由 iOS 與你的 Apple 帳號設定管理。我們不放廣告追蹤、不做行為分析，也不會自動傳送含有個人資料的錯誤紀錄。

資料什麼時候會離開 App

  • 分享班表：App 產生圖片交給你，由你決定傳給誰。內容不含薪資或請假原因。
  • 匯出備份：備份檔加密，需你設定的密碼才能打開；存放位置由你選擇。
  • 匯出薪資彙總 CSV：存放位置由你選擇。

檔案離開 App 後，我們無法知道它們去了哪裡，也無法替你收回。

訂閱與付款

購買由 Apple 處理。我們不會看到或保存你的付款方式與信用卡資訊。App 會向系統查詢目前是否有有效訂閱。

資料保護

薪率、薪資明細、付款紀錄與備份操作需要先通過你 iPhone 的 Face ID、Touch ID 或密碼。這是裝置的保護，不是不同管理者的權限隔離。任何能解鎖這支手機的人都可能看到資料。若手機未設定密碼或生物辨識，這層保護不適用，App 會在畫面上說明。

兒童

本 App 不以兒童為對象，也不會詢問使用者年齡。

刪除資料

App 沒有帳號。刪除 App 可移除手機上的 App 資料；你自行匯出的檔案或由 iPhone 系統建立的備份，需在其存放位置另外管理或刪除。

變更與聯絡

若政策變更，我們會更新本頁與上方日期。有問題請寄信到 mm7382@gmail.com。

English

What we collect

The developer does not collect your app data. The stores, employees, schedules, attendance, leave and payroll information you create are stored by the app on your iPhone. ShiftOK has no developer server or accounts and does not upload this data to a developer cloud.

If you enable iPhone system backup, Apple may back up app data according to your device settings. This is managed by iOS and your Apple Account settings. We do not use ad tracking, behavioural analytics or automatic error reports containing personal data.

When data leaves the app

  • Sharing a schedule: the app makes an image and hands it to you; you choose the recipient. It does not include pay or leave reasons.
  • Exporting a backup: the file is encrypted and requires the password you set. You choose where to save it.
  • Exporting a payroll CSV: you choose where to save it.

After these files leave the app, we cannot know where they went or retrieve them for you.

Subscriptions and payment

Apple handles purchases. We do not see or store your payment method or credit card information. The app asks the system whether there is a current active subscription.

How data is protected

Viewing pay rates, payslips, payment records and using backup features requires your iPhone’s Face ID, Touch ID or passcode. This is device protection, not separate permissions for managers. Anyone who can unlock the phone may be able to see the data. If the phone has no passcode or biometrics configured, this protection does not apply; the app explains this on screen.

Children

This app is not directed at children and does not ask users for their age.

Deleting data

There are no accounts. Deleting the app removes its data from the phone. Files you exported yourself or backups created by the iPhone system must be managed or deleted separately where they are stored.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Questions: mm7382@gmail.com.
