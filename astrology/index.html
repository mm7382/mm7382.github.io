Shilai Astrology — Learn Western astrology in plain language

Shilai Astrology teaches you to read your own Western astrology birth chart through plain-language lessons. Charts use Placidus houses; if you don't know your birth time, the app marks what can't be confirmed instead of inventing an ascendant or houses. A learning tool, not fortune telling. First three lessons free; Traditional Chinese and English.
