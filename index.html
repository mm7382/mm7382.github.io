Plain-language learning apps for Chinese metaphysics and astrology

Four iPhone apps that teach you how to read the I Ching (YiJian), Bazi (Shilai – Bazi), Zi Wei Dou Shu (SHILAI Ziwei) and Western astrology (Shilai Astrology). Each has free beginner lessons, Traditional Chinese and English, no account, and keeps your data on your phone.

這些 App 是學習與反思的工具。醫療、法律、投資與其他重大決定，請諮詢合格的專業人士。
