想學紫微斗數，怎麼挑學習 App？五個評估標準

作者：Wei Chun Chuang・2026-10-15

紫微斗數的 App 不少，但「排出一張十二宮命盤」和「教你讀懂這張盤」是兩回事。如果你想學會自己讀紫微斗數，可以用下面五個標準來比較。這些標準適用於任何一款 App，也包括我自己做的那一款。

學紫微斗數的 App 應該具備什麼？

核心問題是：用了一段時間之後，你能不能自己在命盤上找到命宮、說出命宮裡有哪些主星、知道要參看哪些相關宮位？如果答案是否定的，那它比較像一個查結論的工具，而不是學習工具。

標準一：有沒有循序漸進的系統課程？

紫微斗數的學習有自然的順序：先認識十二宮、地支與星曜，再找命宮與身宮，接著認識主星，最後才學三方四正與大限、流年等時間層次。檢查重點：

  • 課程是否照順序安排，而不是一堆星曜解釋。
  • 每課之後有沒有練習，最好能在自己的命盤上找答案。
  • 有沒有筆記或錯題整理，方便回頭複習。

標準二：解釋有沒有標明古籍出處？

紫微斗數流派多，同一顆星在不同書裡的描述可能差很多。傳統上常被引用的典籍是《紫微斗數全書》。值得信任的 App 會讓你分得出哪些是古籍原文、哪些是現代白話轉述、哪些是作者自己的整理。如果每段解釋都沒有出處，就很難判斷它可信到什麼程度。

標準三：有沒有說明命盤怎麼排出來？

紫微命盤以農曆生日與出生時辰為基礎，先定出命宮，再依規則把各星曜排入十二宮。其中有些細節各派做法不同，例如閏月怎麼處理、時辰交界怎麼算。學習 App 不必要求你手動排盤，但最好說明它採用的規則，並在缺少出生時間時直接告訴你無法排盤，而不是隨便補一個時間。

標準四：是否把結論講成必然？

紫微斗數用宮位和星曜描述一個人的不同面向。如果一款 App 把人生寫成不可改變的結論，或用斬釘截鐵的語氣要你照著做重大決定，你學到的只是標籤，而不是讀盤的方法。比較適合學習的設計，是說明星曜在宮位裡的意義與讀法，把判斷留給你自己。

標準五：出生資料的隱私怎麼處理？

排紫微命盤需要出生年月日時，這些都是私人資料。下載前可以看看：

  • 是否一定要註冊帳號。
  • 資料會不會上傳到伺服器。
  • App Store 的隱私標示與隱私權政策怎麼寫。

怎麼快速判斷一款紫微 App 適不適合自己？

先用免費內容試幾天，然後問自己：我能不能在命盤上指出命宮與身宮？我知不知道命宮裡的主星叫什麼？我讀到的解釋，知不知道出自哪裡？能讓你回答「是」的，就是適合你的學習工具。

常見問題

學紫微斗數一定要先會排盤嗎？
不一定。可以讓工具排好十二宮命盤，先學怎麼讀；但最好選一個會說明排盤規則的工具，讓你知道每顆星為什麼落在那個宮。
沒有出生時間可以學紫微斗數嗎？
可以先學概念，例如十二宮、命宮與身宮、主星。但要排出自己的命盤，需要出生時辰。
挑紫微 App 時，最容易忽略的是什麼？
出處與隱私。建議確認解釋是否標明來源層次、是否需要帳號、資料會不會上傳。

想用 App 練習？

老實說明我自己的 App 怎麼對應這些標準：時來－紫微會依出生資料排出十二宮命盤，再用課程帶你自己讀。前三堂免費，內容是宮位、地支與星曜，命宮與身宮，找出命宮的主星，而且不用先填生日就能上完；每堂課照「生活問題、白話圖解、看自己的命盤、同題練習」四步驟進行，並有筆記與錯題本。每句解釋都標明是古籍原文、白話轉述或本專案自撰，古籍原文只取自《紫微斗數全書》卷一至卷三；不算吉凶。沒有出生時間時，App 會直接說明無法排盤。不需要帳號，資料不上傳伺服器。App 即將上架，適不適合你，建議用上面的標準自己檢查。

How to choose an app for learning Zi Wei Dou Shu: five things to check

By Wei Chun Chuang · 2026-10-15

There are a fair number of Zi Wei Dou Shu apps, but building a twelve-palace chart and teaching you to read it are different things. If you want to learn to read Zi Wei Dou Shu yourself, these five criteria help you compare apps. They apply to any app, including the one I made.

What should a Zi Wei Dou Shu learning app offer?

The core question: after using it for a while, can you find your Life Palace on the chart yourself, name the major stars in it, and know which related palaces to look at? If not, it is closer to a lookup tool than a learning tool.

Criterion 1: Is there a structured course?

Zi Wei Dou Shu has a natural order: the twelve palaces, branches and stars; then the Life and Body Palaces; then the major stars; and only then the "three directions and four positions" and time layers such as decades and years. Check:

  • Do the lessons follow that order, rather than being a pile of star descriptions?
  • Is there practice after each lesson, ideally finding answers on your own chart?
  • Are there notes or a mistakes list for review?

Criterion 2: Are explanations tied to classical sources?

Zi Wei Dou Shu has many schools, and one star can be described very differently from book to book. The text most often cited is the Ziwei Doushu Quanshu. A trustworthy app lets you tell classical passages from modern restatements and from the author's own summary. If no explanation has a source, it is hard to judge how far to trust it.

Criterion 3: Does it explain how the chart is built?

A Zi Wei chart is based on the lunar birth date and the two-hour birth period. The Life Palace is fixed first, and the stars are then placed into the twelve palaces by rule. Some details differ between schools, such as how leap months are handled and how hour boundaries are treated. A learning app need not make you build the chart by hand, but it should state the rules it uses, and if the birth time is missing it should say the chart cannot be built rather than filling in a time.

Criterion 4: Does it present fixed verdicts?

Zi Wei Dou Shu uses palaces and stars to describe different areas of a person's life. If an app writes your life as an unchangeable verdict, or uses absolute language to push you into major decisions, what you learn is a label, not a reading method. A design better suited to learning explains what a star means in a palace and how to read it, and leaves the judgement to you.

Criterion 5: How is your birth data handled?

A Zi Wei chart needs your birth date and time, which are personal. Before downloading, check:

  • Whether an account is required.
  • Whether data is uploaded to a server.
  • What the App Store privacy label and privacy policy say.

How can you tell quickly whether a Zi Wei app suits you?

Try the free content for a few days, then ask yourself: Can I point to my Life and Body Palaces? Do I know the names of the major stars in my Life Palace? Do I know where the explanations I read come from? The app that lets you say yes is the right learning tool for you.

FAQ

Do I need to build the chart myself to learn Zi Wei Dou Shu?
Not necessarily. You can let a tool build the twelve-palace chart and focus on reading, but choose one that explains its rules so you know why each star sits where it does.
Can I learn without my birth time?
You can learn the concepts, such as the twelve palaces, the Life and Body Palaces and the major stars. Building your own chart requires the birth hour.
What do people most often overlook when choosing a Zi Wei app?
Sources and privacy. Check whether explanations show their source layer, whether an account is required and whether data is uploaded.
