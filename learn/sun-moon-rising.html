星盤怎麼看：太陽、月亮、上升

作者：Wei Chun Chuang・2026-10-15

很多人第一次看星盤，只知道自己的「星座」，那其實是太陽所在的星座。一張完整的星盤裡還有月亮、上升與其他行星。太陽、月亮、上升是最常被一起提到的三個位置，也是讀星盤最好的起點。

星盤是什麼？

星盤是依出生的日期、時間與地點，畫出那一刻太陽、月亮與各行星在天空中位置的圖。圓盤外圈是十二星座，內圈分成十二宮。

讀星盤時可以用一個簡單的句型：「什麼（行星）以什麼方式（星座）在哪個領域（宮位）表現」。

太陽星座代表什麼？

太陽大約每個月換一個星座，所以只要知道生日，多半就能知道太陽星座。日常說的「我是獅子座」，指的就是太陽在獅子座。

在占星的語言裡，太陽常用來描述一個人的核心認同、活力來源，以及想成為什麼樣的人。

月亮星座代表什麼？

月亮移動得很快，大約兩天半就換一個星座。月亮常用來描述情緒的反應、內心需要的安全感，以及讓自己放鬆、感到舒服的方式。

同一天出生的人，月亮星座也可能不同，所以要確認月亮，最好有出生時間。

上升星座代表什麼？

上升星座是出生那一刻，東方地平線上正在升起的星座。地球自轉讓上升星座大約每兩小時換一個，實際長短因星座與緯度而不同，所以必須知道準確的出生時間和地點才算得出來。

上升常用來描述一個人接觸新環境的方式、給人的第一印象與外在風格。在常見的宮位制裡，上升也是第一宮的起點。

三個位置要怎麼一起看？

可以把它們想成三個問題：太陽回答「我想成為什麼樣的人」，月亮回答「我需要什麼才安心」，上升回答「我怎麼走進這個世界」。

三者落在不同星座很常見。例如太陽在牡羊、月亮在巨蟹、上升在天秤，可以描述成：核心想主動開創，內心需要親密與照顧，與人接觸時則顯得重視和諧。三個位置互相補充，不需要只挑一個當作「真正的我」。

星座的形容詞從哪裡來？

十二星座可以用元素和動法來理解。四元素是火、土、風、水，描述氣質；三種動法是本位、固定、變動，描述做事的節奏。

  • 火象：牡羊、獅子、射手
  • 土象：金牛、處女、摩羯
  • 風象：雙子、天秤、水瓶
  • 水象：巨蟹、天蠍、雙魚

例如牡羊座是火元素、本位，所以常被形容為直接、主動開始。把星座當作形容詞，套在太陽、月亮、上升上，就是讀盤的第一步。

常見問題

為什麼我覺得自己不像太陽星座的描述？
太陽只是星盤的一部分。月亮、上升和其他行星也參與描述，把三個位置一起看，通常比較完整。
上升星座需要出生時間嗎？
需要。上升大約每兩小時就換一個星座，沒有準確的出生時間和地點，就無法確定。
月亮星座可以只靠生日查嗎？
有時可以。如果當天月亮整天都在同一個星座，生日就夠了；如果月亮在當天換星座，就需要出生時間才能確認。

How to read a birth chart: the Sun, Moon and Ascendant

By Wei Chun Chuang · 2026-10-15

When people first look at a birth chart, the only thing many of them know is their "sign", which is actually the sign the Sun was in. A full chart also includes the Moon, the Ascendant and the other planets. The Sun, Moon and Ascendant are the three positions mentioned most often, and they are the best place to start reading.

What is a birth chart?

A birth chart is a map of where the Sun, Moon and planets were in the sky at the date, time and place of your birth. The outer ring shows the twelve signs; the inner circle is divided into twelve houses.

A simple sentence pattern helps you read it: "what (planet) expresses itself in what way (sign) in which area of life (house)".

What does your Sun sign represent?

The Sun moves into a new sign about once a month, so your birthday is usually enough to know your Sun sign. When someone says "I'm a Leo", they mean the Sun was in Leo.

In astrological language the Sun usually describes core identity, the source of vitality, and who a person wants to become.

What does your Moon sign represent?

The Moon moves fast, changing sign roughly every two and a half days. It usually describes emotional responses, the security a person needs inside, and the ways they relax and feel comfortable.

People born on the same day can have different Moon signs, so a birth time helps confirm it.

What does your Ascendant represent?

The Ascendant, or rising sign, is the sign rising on the eastern horizon at the moment of birth. Because the Earth rotates, it changes roughly every two hours, though the exact length varies by sign and latitude. That is why it needs an accurate birth time and place.

The Ascendant usually describes how a person approaches new surroundings, the first impression they give and their outward style. In the common house systems it also marks the start of the first house.

How do you read the three together?

Think of them as three questions. The Sun answers "who do I want to become?", the Moon answers "what do I need to feel safe?", and the Ascendant answers "how do I step into the world?"

It is common for all three to be in different signs. Take Sun in Aries, Moon in Cancer and Ascendant in Libra: at the core this person wants to initiate, inside they need closeness and care, and when meeting others they come across as valuing harmony. The three complement each other; there is no need to pick one as the "real me".

Where do the sign adjectives come from?

The twelve signs can be understood through elements and modalities. The four elements, fire, earth, air and water, describe temperament; the three modalities, cardinal, fixed and mutable, describe the rhythm of action.

  • Fire: Aries, Leo, Sagittarius
  • Earth: Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn
  • Air: Gemini, Libra, Aquarius
  • Water: Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces

Aries, for example, is cardinal fire, so it is often described as direct and quick to start things. Using each sign as an adjective for your Sun, Moon and Ascendant is the first step in reading a chart.

FAQ

Why don't I feel like my Sun sign description?
The Sun is only one part of the chart. The Moon, the Ascendant and the other planets also contribute, and reading the three positions together usually gives a fuller picture.
Does the Ascendant need a birth time?
Yes. It changes sign about every two hours, so it can't be determined without an accurate birth time and place.
Can I find my Moon sign from my birthday alone?
Sometimes. If the Moon stayed in one sign all day, the birthday is enough; if it changed sign that day, you need the birth time to confirm it.
