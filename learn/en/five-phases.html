What are the Five Phases? How Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal and Water relate

By Wei Chun Chuang · 2026-10-15

The Five Phases are a basic idea in traditional Chinese thought, used in Bazi, Chinese medicine and feng shui. For Bazi learners they are the foundation of chart reading: every stem and branch belongs to a phase, and the Ten Gods are derived from phase relationships. Here is the simplest way to understand them.

What are the five phases?

Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal and Water. The Chinese word "xing" carries the sense of movement and change, so the Five Phases are better thought of as five qualities or states than as five fixed substances. "Five Phases" stresses that they cycle; "Five Elements" is also a common translation.

What is the generating cycle?

Generating means producing or nourishing. The order is:

Wood → Fire → Earth → Metal → Water → Wood

A common way to remember it: wood burns to make fire; fire leaves ash that becomes earth; earth holds metal; metal gathers condensation; water nourishes trees. These are traditional images to help you remember the order.

What is the controlling cycle?

Controlling means restraining or keeping in check. The order is:

Wood controls Earth → Earth controls Water → Water controls Fire → Fire controls Metal → Metal controls Wood

The images: roots break through soil; earth dams hold back water; water puts out fire; fire melts metal; a metal axe cuts wood. A simple check: controlling skips one step in the generating order. Wood generates Fire, Fire generates Earth, so Wood controls Earth.

Is controlling a bad thing?

No. Generating and controlling need each other. With only generation, growth would run unchecked; with only control, the system would stall. Together they keep the five in balance. In Bazi, "control" describes a restraining relationship, not a judgment of good or bad.

What are the common correspondences?

  • Wood: east, spring, green-blue
  • Fire: south, summer, red
  • Earth: centre, the transitions between seasons, yellow
  • Metal: west, autumn, white
  • Water: north, winter, black

These come from the traditional classification system. In Bazi, direction and season matter most because they tie in with the branches and the month.

How are the Five Phases used in Bazi?

Each of the ten stems belongs to a phase: Jia and Yi are Wood, Bing and Ding Fire, Wu and Ji Earth, Geng and Xin Metal, Ren and Gui Water; the twelve branches also have phases. To read a chart, find the Day Master and then check each other character's phase relationship to it:

  • Same as me: the same phase as the Day Master.
  • Produced by me: the phase the Day Master generates.
  • Controlled by me: the phase the Day Master controls.
  • Controlling me: the phase that controls the Day Master.
  • Producing me: the phase that generates the Day Master.

For a Bing Fire Day Master: Fire generates Earth, so Wu and Ji Earth are "produced by me"; Water controls Fire, so Ren and Gui Water are "controlling me". Split by polarity, these five relationships become the Ten Gods.

FAQ

Are the Five Phases the same as the Western four elements?
No. The Western elements are fire, earth, air and water and describe substance and temperament; the Five Phases are Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal and Water and focus on the cycles by which they generate and control one another.
What does it mean if a chart lacks one phase?
A phase missing from the eight characters is a structural feature. Traditionally readers also look at hidden stems in the branches and the chart as a whole before drawing conclusions.
How can I remember the controlling cycle?
Learn the generating order Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water first; controlling skips one step, so Wood controls Earth and Fire controls Metal.
