命宮與身宮有什麼不同？

作者：Wei Chun Chuang・2026-10-15

學紫微斗數，很快會在命盤上看到兩個標記：「命」與「身」。命宮是十二宮之一；身宮卻不是另外一個宮，而是疊在某一宮上的標記。這篇說明兩者怎麼排出、各自代表什麼，以及讀盤時怎麼一起看。

命宮代表什麼？

命宮是讀盤的起點，傳統上用來描述一個人的基本性格、天生的傾向，以及看待事情的方式。十二宮從命宮開始排列，所以命宮也是整張盤的座標原點。

身宮代表什麼？

身宮常被理解為後天的發展方向，也就是一個人隨著經歷累積，越來越重視、投入心力的領域。

有一種常見說法認為，身宮的特質會隨年齡漸漸明顯；不過各家看法不一，初學時把它當成「另一個值得特別留意的位置」就好。

命宮和身宮是怎麼排出來的？

兩者都由出生月份與時辰決定。先從寅宮起正月，順數到出生月份；再從這一宮起子時：

  • 逆數到出生時辰，停下的那一格是命宮。
  • 順數到出生時辰，停下的那一格是身宮。

因為一個逆數、一個順數，兩者通常落在不同的宮位。

身宮會落在哪些宮位？

依照這個排法，身宮只會落在六個宮位：命宮、夫妻宮、財帛宮、遷移宮、官祿宮、福德宮。對照出生時辰如下：

  • 子時、午時：身宮與命宮在同一宮，稱為「命身同宮」。
  • 丑時、未時：身宮在福德宮。
  • 寅時、申時：身宮在官祿宮。
  • 卯時、酉時：身宮在遷移宮。
  • 辰時、戌時：身宮在財帛宮。
  • 巳時、亥時：身宮在夫妻宮。

命宮和身宮要怎麼一起讀？

一個簡單的做法：先讀命宮，了解基本性格；再看身宮落在哪一宮，那一宮所代表的領域，就是這個人可能特別投入的方向。

例如身宮在官祿宮，可以理解為工作與做事方式在他的生活中占有重要位置；身宮在福德宮，則可以多留意精神生活與興趣。命身同宮時，表示先天的傾向與後天的重心集中在同一處。

讀身宮時要注意什麼？

身宮不是第十三個宮位，它只是疊在某一宮上的標記，那一宮原本的意義仍然存在。

另外，不需要拿身宮和命宮比誰比較重要。兩者搭配起來看，再參考各自的三方四正，才能得到比較完整的描述。

常見問題

身宮會不會落在兄弟宮或子女宮？
依傳統排法不會。身宮只會落在命宮、夫妻、財帛、遷移、官祿、福德這六個宮位。
命身同宮是什麼意思？
子時或午時出生的人，身宮和命宮在同一格。這時讀命宮，也同時是在讀身宮的重點。
不知道出生時辰能找到身宮嗎？
不能。命宮與身宮都需要出生時辰才能排出，時辰不確定時，兩者的位置也就無法確定。

What is the difference between the Life Palace and the Body Palace?

By Wei Chun Chuang · 2026-10-15

Soon after you start learning Zi Wei Dou Shu, you notice two marks on the chart: "Life" and "Body". The Life Palace is one of the twelve palaces. The Body Palace is not a separate palace; it is a marker placed on one of the twelve. This article explains how each is located, what each represents, and how to read them together.

What does the Life Palace represent?

The Life Palace is where reading begins. Traditionally it describes a person's basic character, innate tendencies and way of seeing things. The twelve palaces are laid out starting from it, so it is also the chart's point of origin.

What does the Body Palace represent?

The Body Palace is often understood as the direction of later development: the area a person comes to value and put effort into as experience builds up.

One common view holds that the qualities of the Body Palace become more visible with age, but schools differ. As a beginner, simply treat it as another position that deserves special attention.

How are the Life and Body Palaces located?

Both depend on the birth month and the two-hour birth period. Start the first lunar month at the Yin cell and count forward to the birth month. From that cell, start the Zi hour and:

  • count backward to the birth hour: that cell is the Life Palace;
  • count forward to the birth hour: that cell is the Body Palace.

Because one count goes backward and the other forward, the two usually land in different palaces.

Which palaces can the Body Palace fall in?

With this method the Body Palace can only fall in six palaces: Life, Spouse, Wealth, Travel, Career and Fortune and Virtue. By birth hour:

  • Zi or Wu hour: the Body Palace shares the Life Palace ("Life and Body in the same palace").
  • Chou or Wei hour: Fortune and Virtue Palace.
  • Yin or Shen hour: Career Palace.
  • Mao or You hour: Travel Palace.
  • Chen or Xu hour: Wealth Palace.
  • Si or Hai hour: Spouse Palace.

How do you read the two together?

A simple approach: read the Life Palace first to understand the basic character, then see which palace holds the Body mark. The area of life that palace stands for is a direction the person may put particular effort into.

For example, with the Body Palace in the Career Palace, work and ways of getting things done take an important place in that person's life; in the Fortune and Virtue Palace, inner life and interests deserve attention. When Life and Body share a palace, innate tendencies and later focus are concentrated in the same place.

What should you keep in mind when reading the Body Palace?

The Body Palace is not a thirteenth palace. It is a marker on an existing palace, and that palace keeps its own meaning.

There is also no need to decide whether the Body or the Life Palace matters more. Reading them together, along with each one's three directions and four positions, gives a fuller description.

FAQ

Can the Body Palace fall in the Siblings or Children Palace?
Not with the traditional method. It only falls in the Life, Spouse, Wealth, Travel, Career or Fortune and Virtue Palace.
What does 'Life and Body in the same palace' mean?
For people born in the Zi or Wu hour, the Body Palace is in the same cell as the Life Palace, so reading the Life Palace also covers the Body Palace.
Can I find my Body Palace without a birth time?
No. Both the Life and Body Palaces need the birth hour; if the hour is uncertain, their positions are uncertain too.
