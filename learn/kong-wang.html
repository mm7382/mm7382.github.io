空亡是什麼意思？六十甲子的旬空與常見讀法

作者：Wei Chun Chuang・2026-11-23

「你的日柱帶空亡」「這一柱空亡了」——學八字或看六爻卜卦的人常會聽到這句話，但很少有人先把它是怎麼算出來的講清楚。這一頁從頭說一次：空亡是怎麼來的、六旬的對照表、怎麼找自己的空亡、傳統上怎麼讀，以及它和紫微斗數的空宮為什麼是兩回事。

先說明：底下寫的是本站對流傳解讀傳統的整理，不是唯一標準答案。這套語言在本站被當成反思與自我觀察的工具介紹，不主張它能預測事件；醫療、法律、財務決定請依專業意見處理。

空亡是怎麼來的

答案就在干支的結構裡。天干有十個，地支有十二個，兩邊一個配一個往下排，排滿十個就是一旬。十個天干用完了，地支還剩兩個沒配到——這剩下的兩個地支，就是這一旬的空亡，也叫旬空。

所以空亡不是誰規定的吉凶，而是十配十二這件事必然留下的空缺。六十甲子剛好分成六旬，每旬各有自己的兩個空亡。

六旬空亡對照表

旬範圍空亡
甲子旬甲子到癸酉戌、亥
甲戌旬甲戌到癸未申、酉
甲申旬甲申到癸巳午、未
甲午旬甲午到癸卯辰、巳
甲辰旬甲辰到癸丑寅、卯
甲寅旬甲寅到癸亥子、丑

可以自己驗一次：甲子旬從甲子排到癸酉，用掉的地支是子丑寅卯辰巳午未申酉，十二地支裡沒出現的正是戌和亥。每一旬都可以這樣對一遍。

怎麼找出自己的空亡

最通行的做法是以日柱為準，三個步驟：

  1. 找出日柱的干支。不確定的話先看日主是什麼。
  2. 往回推到該旬的第一柱（天干為甲的那一柱），確定日柱屬於哪一旬。
  3. 查上表得到兩個空亡地支，再看年支、月支、時支有沒有落在這兩個字上。

舉例：日柱是丙寅。甲子、乙丑、丙寅，往回第三個是甲子，所以屬甲子旬，空亡是戌、亥。這張盤只要年、月、時三柱出現戌或亥，那一柱就算落空亡。日柱自己通常不算，因為空亡本來就是從它起的。

另外要知道兩件事。第一，部分流派會另外以年柱起一組空亡（常稱年空），和日柱起的那組並看。第二，有些流派再細分真空亡與半空亡，依日干與該地支的陰陽是否相同來分，陰陽相同的視為力量較重。看到不同說法時，先問對方用的是哪一套起法。

傳統上怎麼讀空亡

最常被誤解的一點：空亡不是凶。傳統讀法把它當成「力量不落實」的標記，所以常見的說法是——吉的打折，凶的也減輕。真正要看的是空亡落在哪一柱，因為每一柱代表的生活面向不同：年柱常對應上一輩與早年環境，月柱對應成長背景與手足，時柱對應晚年與子女。空亡落在哪裡，就是在說那個面向比較難抓得實。

空亡也會被解掉。多數流派認為空亡的地支遇到沖或合時會被填實，力量恢復一部分；大運或流年走到那個地支時，也常被當成填實的時機。各家對「填實到什麼程度」的說法並不統一，這是正常的。

六爻卜卦裡的空亡

空亡在六爻卜卦（納甲）裡用得比八字更直接。占卦時先定出當日的旬空，若代表所問之事的那一爻正好落在空亡上，傳統上會讀成「事情還沒成形、時機未到、對方沒放心上」，而不是讀成失敗。等到出空（過了這一旬，或遇到沖合填實）再看，是常見的處理方式。如果你是從易經那邊過來的，可以先看起完卦之後怎麼解讀。

空亡不是紫微的空宮

這兩個詞最容易被混在一起，但來源完全不同：

  • 空亡：來自六十甲子的干支配對，是剩下的兩個地支，屬於八字與卜筮的概念。
  • 空宮：來自紫微斗數十二宮的星曜分布，指某一宮裡沒有十四主星進駐，要借對宮的星來看。

一個看干支，一個看星曜，只是中文剛好都用了「空」字。另外，部分紫微流派也把旬空、截空列入雜曜一起參看，所以同時學兩套的人更要分清自己在講哪一個。

三個常見誤解

  1. 「空亡就是什麼都沒有」：空亡說的是干支配對的空缺，那一柱的字還在、該有的生剋也還在，只是傳統上認為力道虛。
  2. 「帶空亡的人命不好」：每張盤都有自己的兩個空亡地支，三柱中出現一兩個是常態，不是特例。
  3. 「空亡表一定要背」：不用。知道它是十配十二剩下兩個，隨時可以自己推回來。

常見問題

空亡是什麼意思？
空亡又叫旬空。六十甲子以十天干配十二地支，每十個一旬，所以每一旬都會剩下兩個地支配不到天干，這兩個地支就是該旬的空亡。它描述的是干支配對上的空缺，不是吉凶判斷。
怎麼知道自己的空亡是哪兩個地支？
最通行的做法是看日柱屬於哪一旬，再查該旬的空亡。例如日柱是丙寅，屬甲子旬，空亡就是戌、亥；接著看年支、月支、時支有沒有落在這兩個字上。部分流派另外會以年柱起空亡。
命盤裡有空亡是不好的嗎？
傳統讀法不把空亡當成凶。它表示那個位置的力量不夠落實，所以吉的部分會打折，凶的部分也會減輕。要看空亡落在哪一柱、那一柱原本代表什麼，才有解讀的意義。
空亡和紫微斗數的空宮是同一件事嗎？
不是。空亡是干支配對剩下的兩個地支，來自六十甲子；空宮是紫微斗數裡沒有十四主星進駐的宮位，看的是星曜分布。兩個詞都有「空」字，但推算來源完全不同。
空亡可以被解掉嗎？
多數流派說可以。常見說法是空亡的地支遇到沖或合時會被填實，力量恢復一部分；大運、流年走到該地支時也常被視為填實的時機。各家對填實程度的說法並不一致。

What does kong wang (void emptiness) mean in a Bazi chart?

By Wei Chun Chuang・2026-11-23

Anyone reading Bazi or casting with the six lines runs into the phrase sooner or later: this pillar is void. This page explains where kong wang comes from, gives the table for all six ten-day cycles, shows how to find your own, and separates it from the empty palace of Zi Wei Dou Shu.

What follows is this site's synthesis of the inherited interpretive tradition, not a single standard answer. The site introduces this language as a tool for reflection, with no claim that it predicts events.

Where it comes from

The answer is in the structure of stems and branches. There are ten heavenly stems and twelve earthly branches. Pair them one by one and the stems run out after ten, leaving two branches unpaired. Those two leftover branches are the void branches of that ten-day cycle, called kong wang or xun kong.

So it is not a verdict anyone assigned. It is the gap that pairing ten against twelve must leave. The sexagenary cycle divides neatly into six such cycles, each with its own pair.

The table for the six cycles

CycleRangeVoid branches
Jia ZiJia Zi to Gui YouXu, Hai
Jia XuJia Xu to Gui WeiShen, You
Jia ShenJia Shen to Gui SiWu, Wei
Jia WuJia Wu to Gui MaoChen, Si
Jia ChenJia Chen to Gui ChouYin, Mao
Jia YinJia Yin to Gui HaiZi, Chou

You can check it yourself. The Jia Zi cycle runs from Jia Zi to Gui You and uses the branches Zi, Chou, Yin, Mao, Chen, Si, Wu, Wei, Shen and You. The two never used are Xu and Hai.

Finding your own

  1. Write down your day pillar. If that is new, start with what the Day Master is.
  2. Count back to the pillar of that cycle that begins with the stem Jia, which tells you the cycle.
  3. Read the two void branches from the table, then check whether your year, month or hour branch sits on either of them.

For example, a day pillar of Bing Yin counts back Bing Yin, Yi Chou, Jia Zi, so it belongs to the Jia Zi cycle and its void branches are Xu and Hai. In that chart any year, month or hour pillar carrying Xu or Hai is read as void. The day pillar itself is usually excluded, since the reckoning starts from it.

Two further notes. Some schools derive a second set from the year pillar and read both. Others separate a full void from a half void depending on whether the day stem and the branch share the same polarity. When two sources disagree, ask which reckoning they used.

How the tradition reads it

The common misreading is to treat it as bad luck. The tradition treats it as a mark of something not being solid, so favourable readings are discounted and unfavourable ones softened. What carries meaning is which pillar it lands on, because each pillar stands for a different area: the year pillar for the older generation and early environment, the month pillar for upbringing and siblings, the hour pillar for later years and children.

It can also be undone. Most schools hold that a clash or combination involving the void branch fills it in and restores part of its force, and that a luck pillar or annual pillar arriving on that branch does the same. How much is restored is not agreed on.

Void branches in six-line divination

Kong wang is used more directly in six-line divination than in Bazi. The caster first fixes the day's void branches, and if the line standing for the matter asked about falls on one of them, the tradition reads it as not yet formed, the timing not ripe, the other party not giving it thought, rather than as failure. Waiting until the void has passed before reading again is the usual handling. Coming from the I Ching side, start with how to interpret a reading.

Not the same as an empty palace

  • Kong wang comes from the stem and branch pairing of the sexagenary cycle, and belongs to Bazi and divination.
  • An empty palace comes from the star layout of the twelve palaces in Zi Wei Dou Shu, and means a palace with none of the fourteen major stars in it, read by borrowing from the opposite palace.

One looks at stems and branches, the other at stars. Both happen to use the word empty in Chinese. Some Zi Wei schools do also list the void branches among their minor stars, which is one more reason to be clear about which you mean.

Common questions

What does kong wang mean?
They are the two earthly branches that cannot be paired with a heavenly stem inside a ten-day cycle. Ten stems meet twelve branches, so two are always left over. It describes a gap in the pairing, not a good or bad omen.
How do I find my own void branches?
Find which of the six cycles your day pillar belongs to and read off that cycle's two leftover branches, then check whether the year, month or hour branch falls on one of them.
Is a void branch in my chart bad news?
The tradition does not read it as bad. It says the position is less solid, so favourable readings are discounted and unfavourable ones softened. Which pillar it lands on is what carries meaning.
Is it the same as an empty palace in Zi Wei Dou Shu?
No. One is computed from stem and branch pairing, the other from where the fourteen major stars fall. They share the word empty and nothing else.
Can a void branch be cancelled?
Most schools say it can be filled in by a clash or a combination, or by a luck pillar or annual pillar arriving on that branch. Schools disagree on how much is restored.

Check it on your own chart

The Shilai Bazi web version is free in the browser: enter your birth details and it builds the four pillars, so you can find which cycle your day pillar sits in and come back to the table above. There is also an iPhone app, 時來－八字.
