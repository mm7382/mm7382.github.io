星座的四元素與三種模式是什麼？

作者：Wei Chun Chuang・2026-10-15

十二星座看起來要記很多，其實有一個很實用的整理方式：四元素與三種模式。每個星座都正好是「一種元素」加上「一種模式」，四乘三剛好十二。學會這個架構，就能很快抓到每個星座的基本性格。

星座的四元素是什麼？

四元素是火、土、風、水，描述一個星座的氣質，也就是它關注什麼、用什麼方式感受世界：

  • 火象：牡羊座、獅子座、射手座。熱情、主動、重視行動與自我表達。
  • 土象：金牛座、處女座、摩羯座。務實、穩定、重視具體成果與實際感受。
  • 風象：雙子座、天秤座、水瓶座。重視思考、溝通、交流與觀念。
  • 水象：巨蟹座、天蠍座、雙魚座。重視情感、直覺與人與人之間的連結。

傳統上火與風被歸為陽性、外向的元素，土與水被歸為陰性、內向的元素。

三種模式是什麼？

三種模式又稱三種動法或三種性質，描述一個星座做事的節奏：

  • 本位（開創）：牡羊座、巨蟹座、天秤座、摩羯座。擅長開始、發起、帶頭。
  • 固定：金牛座、獅子座、天蠍座、水瓶座。擅長維持、堅持、深化。
  • 變動：雙子座、處女座、射手座、雙魚座。擅長調整、適應、轉換。

三種模式和季節有什麼關係？

這個分類來自北半球的季節。本位星座的起點正好是季節的開始：牡羊座對應春分、巨蟹座對應夏至、天秤座對應秋分、摩羯座對應冬至。固定星座位在季節的中段，季節的特徵最穩定；變動星座位在季節的尾聲，準備轉入下一季。所以本位是開始、固定是持續、變動是轉換。

每個星座怎麼用元素加模式來描述？

把兩個分類疊在一起，就能得到每個星座的基本輪廓。例如：

  • 牡羊座＝火象＋本位：用行動開啟新局面。
  • 金牛座＝土象＋固定：穩定地累積具體的事物。
  • 雙子座＝風象＋變動：靈活地蒐集與交換資訊。
  • 巨蟹座＝水象＋本位：主動建立情感上的安全感。

同樣是火象，牡羊座（本位）偏向起頭，獅子座（固定）偏向持續發光，射手座（變動）偏向探索與轉換。這樣比較，就能看出同元素星座之間的差別。

怎麼用四元素與三種模式讀星盤？

初學者可以做一個簡單的統計：把星盤上太陽、月亮、上升與其他行星所在的星座，分別歸到四元素與三種模式，看看哪一類比較多、哪一類比較少。這能幫你看出整張星盤的整體傾向，例如偏重行動還是偏重思考，偏向開始還是偏向維持。這是觀察的起點，不是對一個人的定論。

把星座當形容詞來用是什麼意思？

與其說「我是某某座的人」，不如把星座當成形容詞：例如「火象的表達方式」「固定星座的堅持」。這樣讀星盤時，就能把行星、星座與宮位組合成一句完整的描述，而不是只貼一個標籤。

常見問題

四元素和中國的五行一樣嗎？
不一樣。四元素是火、土、風、水，描述星座的氣質；五行是木、火、土、金、水，著重彼此相生相剋的循環。兩者來自不同的傳統。
每個星座都只屬於一個元素和一個模式嗎？
是的。十二星座正好是四元素乘以三種模式，每個星座都是唯一的一種組合。
本位、固定、變動哪一種比較好？
沒有高低之分。三種模式只是描述做事的節奏：開始、持續與轉換，每一種都有它擅長的地方。

What are the four elements and three modalities of the zodiac signs?

By Wei Chun Chuang · 2026-10-15

Twelve signs can seem like a lot to remember, but there is a very practical way to organise them: the four elements and the three modalities. Every sign is exactly one element plus one modality, and four times three is twelve. Learn this framework and you can quickly grasp the basic character of each sign.

What are the four elements?

The four elements are fire, earth, air and water. They describe a sign's temperament: what it pays attention to and how it experiences the world.

  • Fire: Aries, Leo, Sagittarius. Enthusiastic and active, valuing action and self-expression.
  • Earth: Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn. Practical and steady, valuing concrete results and physical experience.
  • Air: Gemini, Libra, Aquarius. Valuing thought, communication, exchange and ideas.
  • Water: Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces. Valuing feeling, intuition and connection between people.

Traditionally fire and air are classed as masculine, outward-facing elements, and earth and water as feminine, inward-facing ones.

What are the three modalities?

The modalities, also called qualities, describe a sign's rhythm of action:

  • Cardinal: Aries, Cancer, Libra, Capricorn. Good at starting, initiating and leading.
  • Fixed: Taurus, Leo, Scorpio, Aquarius. Good at sustaining, persisting and deepening.
  • Mutable: Gemini, Virgo, Sagittarius, Pisces. Good at adjusting, adapting and changing course.

How do the modalities relate to the seasons?

The scheme comes from the Northern Hemisphere seasons. Each cardinal sign begins at the start of a season: Aries at the spring equinox, Cancer at the summer solstice, Libra at the autumn equinox, Capricorn at the winter solstice. Fixed signs sit in the middle of each season, when its character is most settled; mutable signs come at the end, as one season turns into the next. So cardinal means beginning, fixed means continuing, and mutable means transition.

How do element and modality describe each sign?

Combining the two gives each sign its basic outline. For example:

  • Aries = fire + cardinal: opening new ground through action.
  • Taurus = earth + fixed: steadily building up tangible things.
  • Gemini = air + mutable: nimbly gathering and exchanging information.
  • Cancer = water + cardinal: actively creating emotional security.

Among the fire signs, Aries (cardinal) leans towards starting, Leo (fixed) towards shining steadily, and Sagittarius (mutable) towards exploring and moving on. Comparing them this way shows how signs of the same element differ.

How can you use elements and modalities to read a chart?

Beginners can make a simple tally: sort the signs of the Sun, Moon, Ascendant and other planets into the four elements and three modalities, and see which are plentiful and which are scarce. This shows the chart's overall leanings, such as action versus thought, or starting versus sustaining. It is a starting point for observation, not a final statement about a person.

What does it mean to use a sign like an adjective?

Rather than saying "I am a such-and-such sign person", treat signs as adjectives: "a fiery way of expressing", "fixed-sign persistence". When reading a chart you can then combine planet, sign and house into a full sentence rather than sticking on a single label.

FAQ

Are the four elements the same as the Chinese Five Phases?
No. The four elements are fire, earth, air and water and describe a sign's temperament; the Five Phases are Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal and Water and focus on cycles of generation and control. They come from different traditions.
Does each sign belong to only one element and one modality?
Yes. The twelve signs are exactly four elements times three modalities, so each sign is a unique combination.
Which modality is best: cardinal, fixed or mutable?
None is better. The modalities simply describe rhythms of action, beginning, continuing and transition, and each has its strengths.
