Shilai Zi Wei — Read your own Zi Wei chart

Coming soon to the App Store. Shilai Zi Wei builds your Zi Wei Dou Shu (Purple Star Astrology) twelve-palace chart from your birth details and teaches you, lesson by lesson, how to read it yourself. The first three lessons can be finished before you even create a chart. Every explanation is labelled as classical passage, plain-language restatement, or the project's own wording; classical lines come only from volumes 1–3 of the Ziwei Doushu Quanshu, checked against the original text.

Who This Is For

  • Complete beginners in Zi Wei Dou Shu.
  • People who have a chart but can't follow the twelve palaces and stars.
  • Learners who want to know where each explanation comes from.
  • Learners in English or Traditional Chinese.

  2. The Life and Body palaces
  3. Finding the major stars in your Life Palace

  2. Time layers: decade periods, minor periods, yearly periods, Dou Jun, and monthly periods

How It Differs from Typical Fortune Apps

  • A course first: it teaches chart reading rather than handing you conclusions.
  • Classical passages come only from volumes 1–3 of the Zi Wei Dou Shu Quan Shu and are checked against the original.
  • Every explanation shows its source layer.
  • No fortune verdicts, no luck-changing advice.
  • No account required; data is not uploaded to a server.

Short Glossary

THE TWELVE PALACES
The chart is divided into twelve palaces, such as Life, Siblings and Spouse, each standing for one area of life.
The Life and Body palaces
The Life Palace is where reading begins; the Body Palace is another key reference point.
Major stars
The main stars in a palace, key to understanding it.
Four positions
Reading a palace together with the three palaces related to it.
Decades and years
Time layers of the chart: decade periods and yearly periods.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there an app for learning Zi Wei Dou Shu?
Shilai Zi Wei builds your twelve-palace chart and teaches you to read it through lessons. The first three lessons are free and need no birth details.
Where should a Zi Wei Dou Shu beginner start?
With palaces, branches and stars, then the Life and Body Palaces, then the major stars in your Life Palace — exactly the three free lessons.
Are the explanations sourced?
Yes. Each line is labelled as classical passage, restatement or the project's own wording; classical lines come from volumes 1–3 of the Ziwei Doushu Quanshu.
Can I use it without knowing my birth time?
A chart can't be generated without it, and the app says so plainly — but the first three lessons work without a chart.
